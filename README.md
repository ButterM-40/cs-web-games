# CS Booth Arcade

Thirteen short browser games: eight action games just for fun, and five that sneak in some computer science. No installation or internet needed.

**Hide games you don't want:** open the **Manage games** tab on the launcher and switch games off. They disappear from the menu on that computer (their scores are kept). **Show all games** brings everything back.

**To start:** double-click `index.html`. Use Chrome or Edge, then press **Full screen** on the launcher.

## Just for fun

| Game | Players | Controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Astro Storm 3D | 1 | Mouse or touch to aim and hold to fire (or arrows + space) |
| Hyper Tunnel | 1 | Left/right arrows or A/D, or hold the left/right side of the screen |
| Sky Stack | 1 | Tap, click or space |
| Trail Duel | 1 vs CPU, or 2 | Pink: WASD or swipe. Cyan: arrow keys |
| Neon Breaker | 1 | Mouse, touch or arrows; click or space to launch |
| Cave Glider | 1 | Hold mouse, finger or space to fly up |
| Cloud Hopper | 1 | Mouse, finger or arrows to steer |
| Puck Clash | 1 vs CPU, or 2 | Pink: mouse, finger or WASD. Cyan: arrows or a second finger |

Trail Duel and Puck Clash are match games, so they have no high-score list. They're the best ones to draw a crowd: two friends on one laptop.

## Running the booth

- Games work with mouse, touchscreen, keyboard or a game controller (Xbox, PlayStation or similar, plugged in or Bluetooth).
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys move between buttons and Enter presses them. Action games keep their own keys (listed in each game).
- **Controller:** D-pad or left stick moves between buttons, A presses. In action games the stick/D-pad moves and A (or RB/RT) fires, jumps or drops. In Puck Clash and Trail Duel the first controller plays Pink and the second plays Cyan. View/Back returns to the launcher. Press any controller button once after a page opens so the browser notices it.
- **Saving initials with a controller:** up/down changes the letter, right adds the next letter, left deletes, A saves.
- An abandoned game returns to the launcher after 90 seconds.
- Scores are saved in that browser on that computer. They survive restarts, but don't move between laptops.
- **Remove a rude name:** hold Shift and click the score on that game's game-over screen.
- **Before the event:** press **Reset all scores** to clear any test scores.
